# Step 1 POI Feature Vocabulary

This note records the point-of-interest (POI) vocabulary used when constructing
the Step 1 spatial representation vector `h`. POI features are aggregated to the
Public Use Microdata Area (PUMA) level before they are used with LODES-derived
features as spatial representation inputs in Step 2.

| Broad activity categories | Coarse activity groups that summarize the functional composition of POIs within a PUMA. | professional services; civil society; healthcare; retail; home services; entertainment and recreation; dining; automotive services; beauty; convenience and grocery stores; transportation services; travel; bars; uncategorized POIs |
