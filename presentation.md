Practice presentations
Everyone will have an opportunity to give a practice presentation to half the class.

Practice presentation details

Purpose

Give a persuasive talk about something from your field or research or research journey that you can make understandable to everyone in the audience

What does 'persuasive' mean?

The audience wants to see you

give a clear introduction that says what the talk is about and why the subject matters (to your audience of classmates from mixed disciplines)
talk to them
try to interest them
give a clear conclusion that restates what you said and why it matters  
Length

max. 3-4minutes

Can I use visuals?

Yes, you can. You may use no more than 1.

Can I speak from notes?

If you don't mind showing you don't care about your audience

Is there a Q & A?

Possibly, at least one question

Is this graded?

The practice presentation offers you an opportunity to practice presentation skills demonstrated in units 5 and 6 and then to reflect on general and individual strengths and weaknesses, all within a supportive and reflective environment of peers.

 

Schedule

early in semester

practice talking informally about your research in small groups
complete self-study activities on talking about and presenting research
choose a topic and a date for your practice presentation and plan for it
request feedback on your presentation plan from your teacher (optional)
middle of semester

give a practice presentation to half the class
audience members watch the presentations and record general observations in a shared text
presenters and audience members reflect on the presentations1 and consider one or two skills they might wish to develop for future presentations
